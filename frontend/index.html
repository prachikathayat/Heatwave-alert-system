Reduce outdoor exposure, stay hydrated, use cooling spaces and regularly check on children, elderly people and outdoor workers.

HEAT SAFETY CENTER

Protect Yourself From Extreme Heat

Practical actions that can reduce your risk during heatwave conditions.

01
💧

Hydration Strategy

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Do not wait until you feel very thirsty. Carry water when travelling outdoors.

02
🕐

Choose Safer Hours

Plan outdoor work, exercise and travel during cooler parts of the day whenever possible. Avoid unnecessary exposure during peak heat.

03
🏠

Keep Your Home Cool

Close curtains or blinds during intense sunlight. Improve ventilation when outdoor air becomes cooler and use fans or cooling systems when available.

04
👵

Check Vulnerable People

Regularly check elderly people, young children, people with limited mobility and others who may be more vulnerable to heat.

05
👷

Protect Outdoor Workers

Schedule rest breaks, provide drinking water, use shaded areas and reduce continuous exposure during extreme heat.

06
👕

Dress for the Heat

Prefer loose, lightweight and breathable clothing. Use a hat or umbrella when outdoor exposure cannot be avoided.

07
🚑

Know Heat Illness Signs

Watch for dizziness, weakness, headache, nausea, heavy sweating, confusion or fainting. These can indicate serious heat-related illness.

08
🚗

Never Leave Anyone in a Car

Never leave children, elderly people or pets inside a parked vehicle, even for a short time. Vehicle interiors can heat up rapidly.

09
🌳

Find Cooling Spaces

Use shaded areas, libraries, community buildings or other available cooling spaces when your home becomes dangerously hot.
